MY APPROACH

More than just
making it work.

For me, development isn't just about making something work. It's about making it work beautifully.

A Shopify store should not feel like a collection of features. It should feel like one thoughtful experience, from the first interaction to the final product detail.

I care about the details: how a product page behaves on mobile, whether a section is flexible for the store owner, whether an app integration feels native, and whether the final result still feels like the brand.

A LITTLE BIT BEHIND THE SCREEN

I didn't start with Shopify.
I found my way to it.

I studied Computer Science, learned how to build things with code, and spent time figuring out where I wanted to take those skills.

Over time, I realized I was most drawn to the part where code becomes something people can actually see and experience. The layout, the interaction, the tiny details and the way a product page feels on mobile.

That naturally pulled me toward frontend development, UI and eventually Shopify. I could take a design, understand the business behind it, write the code, customize the experience and actually see it become a working store.

And that's the kind of work I want to keep doing: not just building websites, but building storefronts that feel intentional, useful and genuinely good to use.
