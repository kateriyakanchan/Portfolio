PROCESS

How we turn an idea into a better Shopify store.

A straightforward process designed to keep your project clear, organized and moving forward - from the first conversation to the final launch.

THE WORKFLOW

A clear path from idea
to storefront.

01

DISCOVER

First, I understand what you need.

Before touching the code, I want to understand your store, your goals and what isn't working.

  • What you're trying to build or improve
  • Your current Shopify setup
  • Your design or visual direction
  • The problems you're facing
  • Your timeline and priorities

The goal: understand the problem before jumping into the solution.

02

PLAN

Turn the idea into a clear plan.

Once I understand the requirements, I'll break the project into clear tasks and define what needs to be done.

  • Page or section planning
  • Shopify theme requirements
  • Design interpretation
  • Content and metafield structure
  • App and integration requirements
  • Development priorities

You'll know what we're building before development begins.

03

BUILD

This is where the store starts taking shape.

I work inside Shopify to turn the plan into a functional storefront.

  • Shopify theme customization
  • Liquid development
  • Custom sections and blocks
  • Product page customization
  • Metafields and dynamic content
  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • App integrations
  • Responsive layouts

The focus is not only on making it work - but making it feel right.

04

TEST

Details matter.

Before considering the project finished, I test the implementation across different screen sizes and check the important interactions.

  • Mobile responsiveness
  • Desktop layout
  • Buttons and interactions
  • Variant behaviour
  • Product information
  • Sections and templates
  • App integrations
  • Visual consistency

Build -> test -> refine.

05

REFINE

Small details make a big difference.

After the first implementation, we review the work and identify anything that needs adjustment.

  • Spacing
  • Typography
  • Alignment
  • Mobile behaviour
  • Content placement
  • UI details
  • Minor functionality changes

The goal is to make the final storefront feel polished rather than simply finished.

06

LAUNCH

Ready to go live.

Once everything has been reviewed and approved, the final implementation is prepared for launch.

  • Final changes are completed
  • Storefront elements are working
  • Responsive behaviour is checked
  • Unfinished or demo content is removed
  • The final implementation is ready

Then it's time to let your store do its thing.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

Clear
communication.

You shouldn't have to wonder what is happening with your project.

Simple updates

I'll keep communication focused on what has been done, what's next and whether anything is needed from you.

Practical solutions

If there are multiple ways to solve something, I'll focus on the option that makes sense for your store - not simply the most complicated one.

Attention to detail

A section looking "almost right" isn't the same as looking right. I'll pay attention to responsive behaviour, spacing, interactions and the small details that make a storefront feel polished.

A collaborative process

Your feedback matters throughout the project. The best result comes from combining your knowledge of the brand with my Shopify and frontend expertise.

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU

Bring the
useful parts.

To keep the project moving smoothly, I'll usually need:

  1. 01Your Shopify store/access where required
  2. 02Design files or references, if available
  3. 03Content, images and product information
  4. 04Clear requirements or examples of what you want
  5. 05Feedback during the review stage

You don't need to have everything figured out before contacting me.
If you're unsure about the technical side, that's okay - that's what I'm here for.
