How much does a Shopify website cost in India?

A useful budget starts by separating the one-time work of building your store from the ongoing cost of running it.

Start with the kind of store you need

A small catalogue with a clear homepage, navigation, product pages and essential policies usually needs less development than a storefront with unusual product options, custom landing pages or advanced integrations. Decide what must be ready at launch and what can wait.

One-time website setup costs

At KANC Studio, scoped Shopify store setup projects are typically quoted at ₹8,000–₹15,000. That can cover theme setup and customization, essential sections, product and collection setup, navigation and responsive storefront checks. The final quote depends on the actual requirements and agreed deliverables—not simply the product count.

Improving an existing store

If your store is already live, you may only need selected fixes. Our Shopify improvement projects are generally ₹2,500–₹7,000 for agreed work such as section adjustments, mobile layout fixes or product-page refinements. Larger custom features require separate scoping.

Don't forget ongoing costs

The Shopify subscription, domain registration, paid themes, third-party apps, payment processing fees and marketing are separate costs. Shopify's published pricing and offers can change; check its official pricing page for current plan costs before budgeting. Paid apps can add recurring expenses.

A sensible launch checklist

Before paying for custom features, confirm the essentials: clear product information, trustworthy policies, simple checkout, working contact details, mobile usability and a way to maintain your catalogue. A lean store that works is a better start than a complicated store with unfinished basics.

What to ask before hiring

Ask for an itemized scope, deliverables, timeline, revision limits and a breakdown of recurring costs. Clarify whether product uploads, copywriting, app subscriptions and launch support are included. Get those decisions documented before development begins.
